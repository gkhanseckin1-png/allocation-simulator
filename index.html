    How the logic works

    Stores. 20 stores in three clusters: A (5 high-traffic stores), B (8 average), C (7 small). Each has its own weekly demand and a size curve S 20% · M 35% · L 30% · XL 15%. Demand follows a season curve (ramp-up, peak, decline) with random noise.

    Equal push. On day one the whole DC stock is split evenly across all stores by size curve. No replenishment, no transfers. A common shortcut when there is no time or data.

    Smart initial allocation. Each store gets enough for the target weeks of cover based on its forecast, never less than the minimum presentation per size. The rest stays in the DC as a reserve.

    Weekly replenishment. Every week the forecast is corrected by actual sales (demand sensing). Stores are topped up to the target cover from the DC, highest-need stores first.

    Transfers. When the DC is empty for a size, stock moves from stores with more than twice the target cover to stores that ran out.

    Markdown. In the check week, if sell-through is below the threshold, price drops 30% for the rest of the season and demand rises. Revenue is counted at €60 full price and €42 after markdown.
